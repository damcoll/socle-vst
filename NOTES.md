First public version of SOCLE, the subtractive bass synthesizer companion of MONOLITHE.

- 2 oscillators (saw / square) + sub + noise, 24 dB ladder filter with drive, 2 ADSR envelopes
- POLY (8 voices), MONO and LEGATO with glide; LFO on pitch, filter or pulse width
- Effects: saturation, Juno-style chorus, tempo-synced ping-pong delay
- MACHINE section (DÉRIVE, POUSSIÈRE), shared with MONOLITHE
- 16 dirty factory presets, save your own as .socle files
- Resizable front panel

Downloads:
- Windows: SOCLE-Windows-VST3.zip
- macOS: SOCLE-macOS-VST3.zip and SOCLE-macOS-AU.zip (not tested on a real Mac yet, feedback welcome)
